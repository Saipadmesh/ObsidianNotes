I wrote, directed, produced, edited and composed music for a short film, and I wanted to talk about it. 

So starting from April of 2025, I had an idea in mind to develop a small script for short film based on the theme of anxiety. I was never really a writer or a director before that, so to create a script that can actually be finished into a short film took a lot of revisions and trials and errors. Finding people to work on this project was easier than I expected because I had already attended theatre workshops by Misfits beforehand, where I met so many like minded artists and friends who were willing to collaborate on film projects. I had prior experience in producing music tracks using FL Studio. I assumed all of this already makes me some sort of capable director, but boy was I wrong. 

Direction is a whole different beast. Even if you can individually contribute amazingly well, in so many ways, a director should lead the team, not be the team. That took me like a few months to figure out.
