# The Process

Introduced in the Lok Sabha for the first reading in 13 March 2026 by Virendra Kumar - Minister of Social Justice and Empowerment
Debated in Lok Sabha on 24 March 2026 (passed amid opposition walkout and protests)
Passed in Rajya Sabha on 25 March 2026
Signed by the president on 26 March 2026
This is an example of a fast tracked law.
Other fast tracked laws passed like this are 
- Farm Laws 2020
- Jammu and Kashmir Reorganization act 2019
- Criminal Law amendment 2018
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023
Fast tracked laws are usually triggered by 
- political decisions
- public outrage
- policy urgency
- administrative tightening

# Notes

During the Lok Sabha debate, a lot of the people opposing the bill and belonging to INC, Shiv Sena, DMK etc. spoke very articulately in English, and gave so many good reasons as to why this bill shouldn't be passed immediately. A lot of their talking points were shared with the internet and Instagram reels that I had seen about this topic. 

The ruling party did not have too many convincing claims as to why this bill should be passed. A lot of their points revolved around "fake" cases of self identification of gender, which could lead to reservations for biological women being claimed by men self identifying as women. Their other point was that the "honorable" Prime Minister would not want to harm the real transgender community.
