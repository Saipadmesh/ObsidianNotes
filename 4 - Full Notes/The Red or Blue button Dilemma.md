In a room with one hundred people, each person is given the choice of pressing one of two buttons - a red one and a blue one. If more than fifty percent of the people pick the blue button as their choice, every person in the room gets to walk away freely. If more than fifty percent of the people pick the red button, then every single person who picked blue dies. What button should each person choose? More importantly, what button would *you* choose?

My instinctive choice would be to pick blue because I don't want anybody to die. And more importantly, I don't want to be responsible for the death of anyone who believes nobody should die.
